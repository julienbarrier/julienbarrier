# hi there, I'm [Julien](https://julienbarrier.eu/)! 👋🏻

I lead the [Low-Dimensional Electronics research
group](https://fkf.mpg.de/lde) at the Max Planck Institute for Solid State
Research in Stuttgart, Germany. We study quantum transport in graphene and other
van der Waals heterostructures to control electrons in low dimensional systems, and
combine it with mid-IR microscopy and near-field photovoltage to probe and control
which defect states gets in our devices.

I previously worked at ICFO in Barcelona and at the University of Manchester.
I am still happy to be at the bench: building setups and measuring low-temperature
electron transport. I also write the code that runs our experiments,
and I am building a group that does the same.

### what I'm working on

- **[mpiLDE](https://github.com/mpiLDE)**: the group's open-source code and
  lab-automation tools.
- **[mesoscopy](https://github.com/mpiLDE/mesoscopy)**: a GUI for running experiments, see the latest blog post below.
- **Building the team**: I am always interested in hearing from motivated
  students and postdocs, see [open positions](http://julienbarrier.eu/blog/2025/12/03/hiring.html).
